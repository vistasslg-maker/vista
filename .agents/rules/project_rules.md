# Project Rules — RAMDHURA VISTA

## Site Structure (Fixed, do not add pages)
The website has exactly 4 pages, in this navigation order:
1. Home
2. About
3. Stay & Discover (everything we provide: stay, dining & experiences, explore Ramdhura, gallery, and getting here, all as sections on this single page)
4. Contact & Booking (always last)
Plus a simple 404 page. No separate Rooms, Dining, Explore, Gallery, Location or legal pages.

## Content Rules
- Zero fabrication. If information is missing, ASK or leave a clearly marked placeholder. Never assume.
- Never invent: room names/types/counts/capacities/prices, availability, reviews, testimonials, ratings, awards, facilities, services, policies, distances, travel times, addresses, coordinates, opening hours, menus, food items, history, owner/family stories, statistics, social accounts, email addresses, booking platforms, payment methods.
- Extra facts found in the brochure go to me for approval; do not adopt them silently.
- All business facts live in /data and /config; never hard-code phone numbers, address, brand strings or amenities in components.
- Do not overengineer: no CMS, no hotel-management features, no fake booking engine.
- Goal: beautiful, trustworthy, fast, responsive, easy to navigate, easy to contact/book. Mobile-first and accessible.

## Image Rules
- No AI-generated photographs of any kind.
- Only use real photographs I supply, real logos/brand assets I supply, or assets I explicitly approve.
- If an image is needed but not supplied, create a clean, clearly labelled placeholder stating exactly what photograph is required. Never silently substitute stock or AI images.

## Do Not
- Invent any information.
- Generate any image.
- Use Lorem ipsum.
- Add pages beyond the 4 listed.
- Start building pages.
- Create documentation, report, or markdown files. Report all audits and results directly in chat.
